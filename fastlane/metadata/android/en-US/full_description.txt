Barrierefreies WebUntis provides students and teachers with fast, accessible access to school schedules, substitution plans, and announcements via WebUntis. Developed in collaboration with students at the LWL-Berufskolleg Soest.

Features:
- Full TalkBack screen reader support with intuitive navigation
- Quick overview of periods, subjects, rooms, and teacher substitutions
- Clear indications of cancellations and timetable changes
- Offline caching for quick access even with unstable internet connectivity
- High-contrast visual modes
- Free, open source, and privacy respecting
